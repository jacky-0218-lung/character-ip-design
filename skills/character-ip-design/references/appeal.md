# Appeal Reference（魅力層：讓極簡角色活起來）

Read this before drawing the hero (Step 9). Subtraction decides WHAT the character is; appeal
decides whether anyone wants to hug it. The two never trade against each other: appeal items
are small, bounded, and always quieter than the super symbol.

## Baby-schema dials（嬰兒圖式：可量化的可愛）

Empirically validated levers (Kindchenschema, Glocker et al.; kawaii research):

- **Head ratio**: 2 頭身 ≈ maximum萌 (stickers, plush); 3 頭身 = poseable. No neck, stubby
  limbs, no fingers — LINE/Sanrio/San-X all comply.
- **Face placement**: cluster small features LOW on a large head; leave a high empty forehead.
  Wide-set eyes. Hello Kitty carries a whole face with eyes + nose + whiskers only.
- **Eye size cap**: bigger eyes read cuter — up to the point they crowd the head outline
  (≈ 15% eye-to-face ratio). Enlarge until just before the eyes touch the contour, then stop.
- **Cheek fullness**: wide face > long face. Roundness = approachability.
- Kawaii doesn't require infant features (Nittono's two-layer model): a dessert, plant, or
  object motif can carry cuteness — so a non-facial super symbol still works.

## The appeal pass — run on every hero draft

（Style note: interpret every rule below *through the locked style*. Style-native traits —
crayon wobble, pixel palette caps, blind-box deadpan, Shōwa faded registration — are the
style, not detail-budget spends; the budget governs charm *added on top*. A pixel palette of
6 colors can still satisfy the 4 roles; a Shōwa palette satisfies "warm accent" with faded
vermillion, not neon.）

### 1. Palette floor（色彩下限：4 個角色）

Dead mascots share one look: a single desaturated mid-tone covering everything. Every
character ships at least these four color roles:

| Role | Rule |
|------|------|
| 主色 main | The body's dominant hue |
| 深一階同色 companion shade | Same hue, one value step darker — flat, used for depth: inner ear, belly patch underside, symbol backside |
| 線/五官深色 feature dark | Warm dark (deep brown/navy of the main hue family), not pure #000 |
| 暖彩點綴 warm accent | Saturated, ≤ 10% of area, on a high-meaning spot: cheeks, tongue, one paw, symbol tip |

60-30-10 as the default area logic. Muted/professional briefs cap saturation on the MAIN coat
only — they still get the accent (沉靜 ≠ 整隻去彩度). The "one hue ≥ 70%, neutral support"
discipline is for color-axis characters only; do not let it leak into other axes.

### 2. Statue check（不做雕像）

Appeal = order + interest. All-order (perfect mirror symmetry, axis-aligned primitives, evenly
spaced features) reads corporate and dead. Break symmetry in exactly ONE place on the hero:
head tilt 5–10°, one bent ear, tail to one side, weight on one leg, symbol pointing one
direction. In poses, always draw a **line of action** — a single imagined curve through the
body; tilt ≥ 8° or clearly asymmetric limbs. Exception: symmetry itself is the declared symbol
(a watchtower, a perfect egg) — then inject interest via the face or the gap instead.

### 3. Detail budget（魅力細節白名單，最多挑 4 項）

The symbol is exempt. Everything else on the base body comes from this list, max 4 items,
each visually quieter than the symbol. For each, the test is "remove it — identity survives,
but warmth drops":

- **Catchlight** — one white dot per eye. OR deliberately none for deadpan/blind-box register
  (Labubu, Molly): this is a *choice* recorded in the sheet, never an omission.
- **Blush** — flat ovals or 2–3 hatch ticks. Cheapest warmth signal in the business.
- **Warm accent patch** — tongue, paw pads, inner ears in the accent color.
- **One flat shade tone** — the companion shade as belly patch / inner-ear / under-symbol.
  Flat fills stay the rule; "flat" never meant "single-tone".
- **Texture ticks** — 2–3 short strokes in ONE spot only: whiskers, stitch marks, a fur tuft.
- **Mouth interior color** — open-mouth smiles get the accent or feature-dark fill.
- **Ground shadow** — one flat ellipse, mocks and scenes only, never in the identity hero.
- **Bold consistent outline** — one weight, warm dark, rounded joins; strongly recommended for
  sticker and kids briefs (it's what makes characters pop at 100 px).

### 4. The gap（一個反差）

One contradiction per character, chosen in Step 8, expressed either visually (cuddly + tiny
fangs; guard tower + sleepy lids) or in persona only (the bio says the brave lion is scared of
loud noises). The gap is what people retell. Two gaps = a mess; zero = forgettable.

### 5. Persona card（性格卡：最便宜的魅力）

Visual minimalism + written personality is the East-Asian IP formula (Sanrio bios, Sumikko's
underdog stories, yuru-chara profiles):

- 3 personality keywords (from intake)
- One-line bio with a hint of struggle or longing — "總是被剩下的炸豬排邊邊" beats three
  accessories. Underdog > perfect.
- One catchphrase in the brand's voice (one sentence, usable as a sticker caption)
- Optional: what it loves / fears (one each)

No fixed narrative epic — Sanrio credits Kitty's longevity to the ABSENCE of a locked story;
the persona is a doorway for projection, not a novel.

## Register dial（萌度／成熟度）

From intake, pick the register consciously:

- **幼齡可愛** — 2 頭身, big catchlight eyes, blush, bold outline, bouncy poses
- **溫暖日常** — 2–2.5 頭身, smaller eyes, soft palette + warm accent, relaxed poses
- **冷面成熟 (deadpan)** — sparse face, no catchlight (deliberate), muted coat + one sharp
  accent, still poses; humor comes from the gap and captions (engineer-friendly registers:
  Octocat, Go gopher live here)

B2B ≠ colorless: a deadpan character still gets its accent and its gap — "professional" is a
register choice, not a saturation deletion.

## Anti-slop checklist（最後過一次）

- Would this survive as a crane-game plush? (silhouette + palette test in one question)
- Is there ONE thing a viewer retells tomorrow? (symbol or gap)
- Does it look like a specific designer made it, or like any AI could have? If the latter:
  the fastest fixes are a stronger gap, a warmer accent, one asymmetry, a better bio.
- Are all three of your last three characters "one mass + dot eyes"? Rebuild this one with a
  different body construction.
