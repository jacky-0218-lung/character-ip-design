# Worldbuilding & Character Depth Reference（世界觀與角色深度設定）

Read this at Phase 1 Step 3, after the market report and before any drawing. The industry
builds meaning before form: BT21's process was sketches → personalities/values → a narrative
universe → THEN final design; San-X generates ~200 concepts a year through internal
competitions and ships 1–2 — the funnel kills concepts at the MEANING stage, not the drawing
stage. A character with no interior is wallpaper with a face.

## First decision: narrative doctrine（先選教義）

Two proven doctrines — choose one explicitly and record why:

- **Sanrio 極簡投射型**: no fixed story; the character is a projection screen (Kitty has no
  mouth so "she looks happy when people are happy"; Sanrio credits her longevity to the
  absence of a locked narrative). Choose when: mass-market, licensing-first, long-horizon.
- **San-X 微敘事型**: small, specific, emotionally pointed backstories (Tonkatsu the leftover
  cutlet edge "1% 肉 99% 油，總是被剩下"; Rilakkuma's kept mystery — a zipper on the back,
  contents unknown). Choose when: community-driven, empathy/治癒 positioning, content-led
  growth. **Kept mystery** is a tool of this doctrine: document 1 thing the audience is never
  told.

Minimal doesn't mean empty and narrative doesn't mean lore-dump — both doctrines cap the
written world at what serves projection or empathy.

## 世界觀設定 template（世界觀 bible, 1 page max）

```
## 世界觀
一句話設定      （where/what this world is — "被剩下的東西聚集的角落"）
世界規則 3–5 條 （physics of the premise: what exists, what can't happen, tone boundaries）
基調            （治癒/荒謬/懷舊/熱血… + 幽默的來源）
主角在此世界的位置
配角掛鉤 2–4 個 （supporting cast hooks with one-line roles — future 第二彈 material）
Kept mystery    （微敘事型才有：那個永遠不說破的謎）
```

## 角色深度設定 template（character deep profile — the analysis the design must obey）

```
## 角色設定分析
價值觀          （what it believes — 1 句）
渴望            （what it wants and can't quite have — drives the gap）
害怕            （1 concrete fear, ideally mundane）
習慣/怪癖 2–3 個（behavioral, drawable in stickers）
關係            （to the cast, to the audience — "牠怎麼看你"）
說話方式        （tone of voice: 句長、語尾、絕不說的詞）
行為規則        （what it ALWAYS does / NEVER does）
禁忌清單        （San-X practice: e.g., 不碰酒/菸/暴力/政治 — write the IP's own taboo list）
反差（the gap） （exactly one — the single contradiction from the design method）
口頭禪          （1 句，能直接當貼圖文案）
```

Every later artifact must be derivable from this sheet: a sticker set is 習慣+口頭禪 drawn;
the at-work scene is 渴望 staged; the persona card in the design sheet is this profile
compressed.

## 系列規劃（series & lineup — design the second wave into the first）

- **Lineup convention**: blind-box economics run on 12 regular + 1 secret (1:144 odds, POP
  MART standard); sticker economics run on 8–24-sticker sets with 混合禮貌用語. Even if the
  first release is one character, define the **variant axes** now: emotions (情緒彈), seasons,
  jobs, collab slots.
- **Extensibility test**: list 第二彈/第三彈 themes that need NO new symbol — if you can't
  name three, the symbol or world is too narrow.
- **Cast expansion**: the 配角掛鉤 above are the pipeline; San-X and Sanrio both grow by
  cast, not by redesigning the lead.

## Naming system

Name the lead for the symbol or the gap, never the industry. Define the naming *system* for
the cast (音感 family: 疊字/擬聲/尾韻), so future characters sound related. All candidates go
through the trademark quick screen (market-research.md §5) before being presented as final.
